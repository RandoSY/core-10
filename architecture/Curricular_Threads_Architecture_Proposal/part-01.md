Purpose and Design Intent
This document captures an emergent curriculum architecture whose purpose is practical scientific
capability. It is intended to be inexpensive enough for broad use, coherent enough to teach durable
understanding, web-extensible enough to outlive any particular hardware kit, and AI-ready enough to adapt
experiments and reasoning to the learner and the physical problem at hand.
    The legacy goal is not to preserve a collection of activities. It is to preserve a way of
    understanding and administering the physical conditions of life.

What this proposal changes
 • CORE 10 remains the common scientific root rather than becoming another parallel subject.
 • Kitchen Inquiry Lab, One Cup Chemistry, and NOTHING IS FREE remain sibling branches with distinct
    intellectual ownership.
 • Experiments are classified as CANONICAL, REVISIT, or SYNTHESIS so reinforcement is deliberate and
    duplication is reduced.
 • Biology / living systems and SDL are cross-cutting threads, not extra competing branches.
 • The Personal Energy Laboratory becomes a synthesis environment that requires prior knowledge instead
    of reteaching it.
 • The web becomes the default home for explanation, data, simulation, scenario management, AI
    interaction, and preserved experiment records.
 • A deliberately small set of physical anchor experiments remains because some knowledge depends on
    contact with imperfect reality.

1. The Architecture at a Glance
   One root. Three disciplinary branches. Two cross-cutting threads. One integrative
   application.

Root: CORE 10
CORE 10 owns the common language of inquiry: measurement, quantity, uncertainty, proportionality,
graphs, conservation, models, and experimental reasoning. Later branches assume this foundation; they do
not restart it.

The progression
   Encounter → Measure → Explain → Reuse → Integrate → Decide
This progression is the core anti-duplication mechanism. A phenomenon may reappear, but its intellectual job
must change each time.

2. Reinforcement Without Duplication
    Repeat the concept when useful. Do not repeat the experiment unless the second
    encounter produces a genuinely new intellectual result.

    Administrative rule: if two activities use the same apparatus, same measurement,
    same graph, and reach the same conclusion, merge them or explicitly reuse the
    earlier experiment.

The persistent experiment record
Each canonical experiment should produce a reusable digital record: raw data, metadata, calibration notes,
graph, interpretation, uncertainty, and conclusions. Later activities should be able to reopen that record
instead of physically recreating the same event without purpose.
 Record element                                               Why preserve it
 Raw measurements                                             Allows re-analysis with new questions.
 Context / metadata                                           Preserves conditions, apparatus, units, and assumptions.
 Calibration / uncertainty                                    Keeps the data grounded in measurement reality.
 Learner interpretation                                       Makes conceptual growth visible over time.
 AI annotations                                               Allows later critique, comparison, and adaptive follow-up.
