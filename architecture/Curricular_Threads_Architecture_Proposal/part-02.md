5. Personal Energy Laboratory: The Integrative Application
    The Personal Energy Laboratory should contain almost no introductory science of its
    own. It is where prior knowledge becomes operational competence.
Central question: How do I maintain viable human life when energy is finite?
 Requirement                                              Knowledge recruited
 Stay warm                                                Thermodynamics, insulation, heat pumps, thermal storage,
                                                          physiology.
 Stay cool                                                Radiation, shading, humidity, evaporation, ventilation,
                                                          refrigeration, physiology.
 Stay transported                                         Batteries, motors, Wh/mile, route choice, charging, reserve.
 Stay fed                                                 Photosynthesis, metabolism, preservation, refrigeration,
                                                          cooking, water.
 Stay hydrated                                            Water chemistry, solutions, purification, pumping, thermal
                                                          demand.
 Stay powered                                             Solar generation, storage, forecasting, load scheduling, peak
                                                          power.

The learner is not asked to rediscover watts, heat capacity, or battery capacity. The learner is asked to make

    systems, measurement, economics, and then administer the conditions of life under
    constraint.
