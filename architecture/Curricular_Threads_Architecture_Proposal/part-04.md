                                                inside a finite energy envelope.
What moves to the web?                          Explanation, datasets, simulation, graphing, scenarios,
                                                experiment design, AI interaction, and preserved records.
What remains physical?                          A small set of anchor experiences where handling matter,
                                                measuring imperfectly, and confronting real heat, motion, noise,

                                                hardware and AI, and a browser-accessible learning path.

   experimental evidence, and the learner’s ability to reason from physical reality.
