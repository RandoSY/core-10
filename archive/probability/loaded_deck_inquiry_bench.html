What the graph means

The fair-deck distribution is centered near n × 0.50. The loaded-deck distribution is centered near n × 0.75. A risk factor does not guarantee an outcome; it shifts the population distribution.
The smoking analogy is about population risk. One person’s outcome is never enough to prove causation. Repeated observations across large groups reveal whether the deck has been loaded.
