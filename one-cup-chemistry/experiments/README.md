## Archival and teaching status

- 15 standalone teacher guides presently visible in the default branch; all 20 units are present in the complete compendium and original archived manual.
- OCC-02 Newton Cooling is the connection to LAB-12; see the [LAB-12 dashboard](https://github.com/RandoSY/software-defined-laboratory/blob/main/platforms/lab12-pdp12-linc/v1.2/dashboard/lab12_dashboard.html).
- The historical v0.2 manual labels conductivity as OCC-07 and the revised soap-film Avogadro activity as OCC-17. Do not confuse the experimental IDs with separately bundled publication filenames.
- The **formatted PDF, editable DOCX, raw measurements and host/target validation** must be preserved and labeled separately. Their mere mention in the manual does not mean they have been released.
- See [Project Atlas preservation baseline](https://github.com/RandoSY/project-atlas/blob/main/baselines/CORE10_CORE12_PUBLIC_PRESERVATION_BASELINE_2026-11-03.md) for missing original binaries, SHA-256 and the wider CORE10/CORE12 registry.
